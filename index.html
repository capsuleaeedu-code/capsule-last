About Rabindranath Tagore University

Your degree is awarded by Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU), which describes itself as India’s first skill-based private university.

RNTU was established in 2010 by the Rabindranath Tagore Group. It focuses on skill-based education, research and strong industry linkages, with a presence in Madhya Pradesh and Jharkhand, India.
