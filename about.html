About Capsule

An education consultancy and learning hub in Abu Dhabi, with an administrative presence in India.

Who we are

Capsule Educational Institute is a premier educational consultancy and learning hub headquartered in Abu Dhabi, UAE, with a dual administrative presence in India.

For nearly a decade, we have helped ambitious students and working professionals across the GCC move forward through accredited higher education and career-focused learning.

We connect learners with respected universities across Asia and Europe, giving them access to recognized undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, vocational certifications and upskilling programs.

Our vision

To be a leading education facilitator in the Middle East and beyond, known for making quality higher education accessible and building a future-ready workforce through world-class university partnerships.

Our mission

To give every learner tailored academic counseling, a transparent admissions process, and flexible, globally recognized degree programs that lift their careers in a changing economy.
