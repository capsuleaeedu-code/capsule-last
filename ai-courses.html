AI Foundations Course

The AI Foundations Course introduces learners to practical artificial intelligence tools and techniques. It is designed for beginners who want to explore how AI can support their studies, work and daily activities.

You do not need previous coding experience to begin. Lessons focus on understanding AI tools, writing useful prompts, checking information and applying what you learn through practical activities.

Course Duration

42 hours

14 sessions, with 3 hours per session.

Suggested Schedule

Two sessions per week over approximately eight weeks.

Contact us to confirm the upcoming schedule.

Who Can Join?

Beginners aged 16+, teachers, office staff, small business owners, parents and career changers.

Entry Requirements

No coding experience required. Learners should have access to a laptop and an email account.

What You Will Learn

AI Fundamentals

Understand introductory AI concepts and explore how AI tools can be used for common tasks.

Prompt Writing

Practise writing clear instructions and improving prompts to get more useful AI responses.

Research and Verification

Use AI to support research while checking facts, sources and the reliability of generated information.

Content Creation

Explore AI-assisted writing, editing, brainstorming and content development.

Knowledge and Documents

Learn introductory workflows for working with documents, notes and knowledge resources.

AI for Everyday Productivity

Practise using AI tools to assist with planning, communication, organisation and routine tasks.

Tools Covered

Learners will be introduced to a selection of AI tools and applications, including:

AI Assistants

ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini

Research and Knowledge

Perplexity and NotebookLM

Design and Visual Content

Canva and AI image-generation tools

Audio and Video

AI video-generation tools and ElevenLabs

Writing and Editing

Grammarly and AI-assisted editing workflows

Tool access, features and availability may depend on each provider's account plans and terms.

Practical Learning and Assessment

The course includes practical activities to help learners apply the techniques covered during training.

Practical Activities

Work through exercises involving prompts, research, writing, content creation and productivity tasks.

Portfolio Development

Build a collection of at least nine practical samples demonstrating your learning and use of AI tools.

Practical Examination

Complete a timed practical assessment to demonstrate the skills covered in the course.

Certificate

Learners who meet the course requirements receive a Capsule Certificate.
